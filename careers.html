Contractor Requirements

To maintain the highest quality for our customers, we look for experienced detailers who are ready to hit the ground running.

  • Pass a full criminal background check
  • Have a working vehicle you can use to commute to the clients' addresses
  • Ability to accept at least 2 jobs per week
  • You've detailed at least 20 cars in the past on a professional basis (for an employer or for yourself)
  • You have all required supplies (Including a shampoo extractor)
