Detail Savings Club Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: November 18, 2026  |  Provider: Michie Auto Detailing LLC

Support: (405) 295-5189  |  andrew@michieauto.com  |  michieauto.com

By enrolling in the Detail Savings Club (the "Club") or completing checkout for a membership-discounted appointment, you ("Member," "you," or "your") agree to the following Terms & Conditions with Michie Auto Detailing LLC ("we," "us," or "our"):

1. Membership Pricing & Automatic Recurring Billing

  • Monthly Fee: Membership in the Detail Savings Club costs $39.99 per month (plus any applicable taxes).
  • Initial Checkout: When joining while booking a detail appointment, your initial checkout payment includes today's discounted detail package price plus $39.99 for your first month of membership.
  • Automatic Renewal: By subscribing, you authorize Michie Auto Detailing LLC and our payment processor (Stripe) to automatically charge $39.99 to your payment method on file every month on your monthly billing anniversary until you cancel.
  • No Long-Term Contract: There is no minimum commitment or lock-in contract. You may cancel at any time.

2. Member Benefits

While your membership is active and in good standing, you receive:

  • 50% Off Mobile Auto Detailing: A 50% member discount off our standard quoted rates on mobile detailing services (subject to a $90 minimum service rate per appointment and standard rounding).
  • $20.00 Monthly Detail Credit: Each paid monthly billing cycle adds $20.00 in Detail Credit to your member account.
  • Credit Rollover: Unused Detail Credits roll over from month to month for as long as your membership remains continuously active.
  • No Cash Value: Detail Credits have no cash value, are non-transferable, cannot be redeemed for cash, and may only be applied toward eligible detailing services performed by Michie Auto Detailing LLC.

3. Eligibility, Active Status & Service Requirements

  • Active Membership Required at Service: Your Club membership must be active and paid in full at the time you book and on the date your detail service is performed. If you cancel your membership prior to your scheduled appointment date, standard non-member pricing will apply to that appointment.
  • Service Area & Mobile Access: Services are mobile and subject to availability within our active service areas (including Oklahoma City, OK and Dallas–Fort Worth, TX). Members must provide safe, accessible parking and permission to detail the vehicle at the service location.
  • Personal Use: Membership discounts apply to personal vehicles owned or regularly driven by the Member and their immediate household. Commercial fleets, dealerships, and rideshare/rental fleets require a separate commercial agreement.

4. Cancellation Policy & Forfeiture of Credits

  • How to Cancel: You may cancel your Detail Savings Club membership at any time with zero cancellation fees by calling or texting us at (405) 295-5189, emailing andrew@michieauto.com, or using your Stripe Customer Portal link (if applicable).
  • Billing Cutoff: To avoid being billed for the next month, please submit your cancellation request at least 24 hours before your next monthly renewal date.
  • Immediate Forfeiture of Unused Credits: Because credits are a loyalty benefit of active membership, all unused $20/month Detail Credits are forfeited immediately upon cancellation. If you cancel and later rejoin, forfeited credits cannot be restored.
  • No Partial Refunds on Membership Fees: Monthly $39.99 membership charges are non-refundable once billed, and we do not provide prorated refunds for partial months. You will continue to enjoy Club benefits through the end of your already-paid billing cycle unless otherwise noted.

5. Appointment Cancellations & Refunds

  • Detail Appointments: Once a detail appointment is booked and paid, rescheduling requests do not incur rescheduling fees. Cancelled appointments receive a 100% service credit valid for 12 months toward a future appointment. No cash refunds are issued for cancelled appointments or completed services.
  • Satisfaction: If you have any concern with your service, please notify our team at (405) 295-5189 before the detailer leaves or within 24 hours so we can address it promptly.

6. Failed Payments & Updating Your Card

  • If a monthly $39.99 renewal payment fails or your card expires, Stripe may automatically retry the charge and send you an email with a secure Stripe-hosted link to update your payment method.
  • While a payment is past due, Club benefits (including the 50% discount and access to accumulated Detail Credits) are temporarily paused until payment is resolved.
  • If payment remains unresolved after Stripe's retry attempts, your subscription may be automatically cancelled and any accumulated Detail Credits forfeited.

7. Changes to Terms or Pricing

We reserve the right to update these Terms & Conditions or adjust membership pricing/benefits in the future. If we ever change the $39.99/month membership rate or make material changes to these terms, we will notify active members via email or SMS at least 48 hours in advance so you may cancel before the changes take effect.
