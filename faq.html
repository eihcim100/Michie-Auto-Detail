What is Michie Auto Detailing?

Michie Auto Detailing LLC is a premium mobile auto detailing service based in Oklahoma City, OK. We bring the showroom shine directly to your doorstep, whether you're at home or work. Our focus is on convenience, quality, and transparent pricing via our AI Quote Tool.

Are you local?

Absolutely! Michie Auto Detail has been exclusively operating in the greater Oklahoma City area since 2023. We are on site, local, and we train and manage our own detailers to ensure an excellent experience.

How much does a detail cost?

Every vehicle is unique, so we've moved away from generic pricing. To get an accurate, custom price for your car, truck, or SUV, use our free AI Auto Detail Quote tool. It takes less than 5 minutes and provides a price based on your vehicle's specific condition.

Why do you require payment in advance?

Our availability is limited, and we operate on a strict schedule to ensure every client gets the attention they deserve. When an appointment is booked, we turn down other potential clients seeking that same time slot. To block off that window exclusively for you, we require payment to be secured before the detail starts. We understand plans change, which is why all of our detail packages include one free reschedule.

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve the entire greater Oklahoma City metropolitan area, including Oklahoma City, Norman, Edmond, Moore, Bethany, Yukon, Mustang, and Midwest City.

Do you include mold removal in your service?

Yes, we do! We specialize in comprehensive interior cleaning, including mold remediation. To get a quote for a mold package (which often covers pet hair and smoke odors as well), please visit our AI Quote Portal.

Do you remove smoke smell?

Yes. Removing smoke odor is one of our specialties. This service is typically included with our deep interior packages. You can select "Smoke Odor Removal" as an option when using our AI Scanning Tool.

Do you offer pet hair removal?

Absolutely! Pet hair removal is a standard part of our comprehensive cleaning. We don't believe in massive surcharges for your furry friends. See exactly what is covered for your car at quote.michieauto.com.

Do I need to provide water or electricity?

Yes, we use the client's water and electricity. This allows us to keep our operating costs low and pass those savings directly to you, providing premium service at a better value than competitors with heavy mobile rigs.

How long does a detail take?

Most premium details take between 2 to 4 hours. The specific duration for your vehicle will be estimated during your AI quote process.
